# mstt_insynch_70_20_35_60, mstt_insynch_70_30, mstt_dnns, mstt_dnns_2nd_exit (lane M2, 20 Nov 2026)

Source: Peter's Window on WallStreet systems, Trading Tech. PDF p.6 (dnns), p.9 (insynch). The InSync Index components (CCIInS, BOLInS*, EMVInS*, MACDInS*, MFIInS, PDOInS*, ROCInS*, RSIInS, STO%kInS, STO%dInS) ARE printed on PDF p.1 (Norm North, TASC Jan 1995; formulas by Barry Millman); the earlier hold ("not printed") was wrong. They are added to the mstt Fml table as printed (legacy If(a,op,b,t,f) written If((a op b),t,f), as msadd does; "ROCInSS Index" read as ROCInSS). slope() is the source's own "Slope of a Line" (p.44).
New built-ins (msws.rs), MetaStock Pro 8.0 manual (text in authors/lanes-1120/M2w/ms8.txt): mfi(n) p.483, emv(n,method) p.465, dpo(n) p.460. Labelled unstated details (review of 20 Nov): insynch cards 2 - MFI average price = (H+L+C)/3; DPO(n)[i] = Close[i] - SMA_n[i + n/2 + 1] (future-shifted as the manual says "the last x/2+1 periods have no value"; read only through Ref(...,-10)). dnns cards 4 - those two plus the SAR start bars and slope(x,3) as the 3-bar least-squares slope (both counted on the slope+binary cards). NOT counted: EMV volume in 10,000s - the cards use only the sign of EMV and the scale is a positive factor, so it cannot change a signal. UNVERIFIED: no printed results.
Causality guard (msws.rs dpo): the engine tracks the sum of Ref(.,-k) offsets around the expression being evaluated (it is part of the expression cache key) and refuses dpo(x) unless that sum is >= x/2+1; a direct use or a shorter Ref returns an error instead of reading future bars. Checked in the prefix test below.
