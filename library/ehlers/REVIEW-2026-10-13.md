# Ehlers daily automatic systems: bounded source review

Two existing daily systems passed independent source review under explicit
research contracts: `ehrs_sinetrend` and `ehmd_mama`. Their existing method IDs
are retained; no indicator, optimized threshold, direction variant, instrument
run or chapter is counted as an additional algorithm. The new algorithm count
is zero. This candidate checkout
does not publish results or certify original TradeStation study performance.

The supplied local sources are *Rocket Science for Traders* (2001), the
*MESA Adaptive Moving Averages* article in Stocks & Commodities 19:9 (2001),
and selected MESA articles/manuals. The private job directory records their
SHA-256 hashes. Rocket Science is a two-page-spread scan: PDF page62 contains
printed pp.118–119, PDF63 pp.120–121, PDF64 pp.122–123, and PDF66 pp.126–127.
The selected spreads were inspected as rendered images. No source scans,
extracted book text, raw prices or generated result JSON belong in Git.

| ID | Source | Inputs and defaults | Orders and positions | Instrument application |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ehrs_sinetrend` | Rocket Science printed p.119; Fig.12.1 pp.120–123 | Actual daily midpoint `(H+L)/2`; `cyc_part=1`; configurable research `max_bars_back=50` | Trend and cycle signals described below. | `ehmd_mama` | MAMA article PDF3–4, printed pp.32–33; source sidebar and explicit long-only crossover test | Actual stock daily midpoint; `fast_limit=.5`, `slow_limit=.05`; configurable research `max_bars_back=50` | MAMA crossing over FAMA enters long; crossing under exits to flat. Both use the source Hilbert homodyne discriminator: FIR weights
`.0962/.5769/-.5769/-.0962` at lags0/2/4/6, with gain
`.075*Period[1]+.54`; delayed InPhase and Quadrature components; phasor
addition; `.2/.8` smoothing of I2/Q2 and Re/Im. Period is computed from
`360/ArcTangent(Im/Re)` when both components are nonzero. The source's
sequential1.5/.67 prior-period limits,6–50 clamp, `.2/.8` period smoothing
and `.33/.67` SmoothPeriod smoothing remain in their printed order.
Trigonometry uses degrees with explicit Rust/Python radian conversion.
Ratio arctangent is retained; it is not replaced with `atan2`.

SineTrend computes a DFT of SmoothPrice over
`IntPortion(SmoothPeriod+.5)`. It keeps the source RealPart/ImagPart
checks, phase corrections of+90 and+360/SmoothPeriod, the+180 correction
for negative ImagPart, and a single-360 correction above315. DCSine and
LeadSine have a45-degree offset. ITrend is the midpoint mean over the
cycle window; Trendline is its4/3/2/1 weighted mean, with current midpoint
substitution while CurrentBar<12.

Trend begins as1 on each evaluated bar. An inline phase-sine crossing
resets DaysInTrend and sets Trend0; DaysInTrend then increments. Less than
half a measured cycle since the crossing, or a strictly bounded .67–1.5
dominant-cycle phase rate, sets Trend0. An absolute SmoothPrice/Trendline
separation of at least1.5% overrides this to Trend1. When Trend changes
from0 to1, an existing short reverses if SmoothPrice>=Trendline, and an
existing long reverses if SmoothPrice<Trendline. SmoothPrice/Trendline
over/under crossings give long/short orders during Trend1. LeadSine/DCSine
over/under crossings give long/short orders during Trend0. The earlier
warmup is used only for indicator state, with the research account flat
at its chosen start.

MAMA retains Phase when I1 is zero, otherwise setting it to
`ArcTangent(Q1/I1)`. DeltaPhase is `Phase[1]-Phase`, floored at1;
alpha is FastLimit/DeltaPhase, bounded by SlowLimit and FastLimit.
MAMA uses alpha and FAMA uses half alpha. Source zero seeds are preserved.
The article explicitly identifies a long-only100-stock crossover study;
MAMA-above-FAMA as long and the opposite crossing as exit are the stated
interpretation of that crossover. No short reversal is introduced.

## Startup, fills and assumptions

The reviewed sources do **not** print the original MaxBarsBack platform
setting. Fifty is an explicit configurable research default, not a
recovered author setting or proof of original-platform parity. The runner
skips that many actual bars before CurrentBar1; calculations begin only
at CurrentBar6, index55 for the default. All source Vars stay zero before
their assignment gate. Earlier actual price history remains available to
Price references; it is not used to seed recursive Vars from prices.
MaxBarsBack must be a finite integer and cover the largest source window
reference. Reviewed parameter extensions above CycPart1 require a larger
declared history bound. Legacy variants retain their authoritative schemas,
startup and calculations; those variants are outside this admission batch.
The runtime rejects nonfinite/unknown parameters, fractional MaxBarsBack,
insufficient history settings and SlowLimit>FastLimit. Readiness also
checks that actual, valid recursive warmup precedes the selected start.

An error in the earlier implementation initialized LeadSine to sin45
before the source assigned it. DCSine and LeadSine now remain zero during
startup. The DaysInTrend predicate still uses the historical inline
functions `Sine(DCPhase)` and `Sine(DCPhase+45)`, whose zero-phase values
are0 and sin45. Those two histories must not be conflated.

Crossings require a current strict inequality and the most recent earlier
unequal relation to have the opposite sign; equality stretches are
skipped. An all-equal zero history does not create a crossing when a first
positive value appears. These are the declared TradeStation crossing
semantics exercised by the independent oracle and boundary tests.

All orders use the next observed open. Signals on the last observed bar
remain unfilled. One position per symbol is tracked without pyramiding
or pooled allocation. An existing terminal position remains open and is
marked at the last close; it is excluded from closed-trade statistics.
The ledger records include that open mark, distinctly labelled from a
filled exit. No end-of-window close order is manufactured.

Rocket Science p.119 describes Fig.12.1 as a complete theoretical system
before adding a USD1000 money-management stop for its Treasury-bond
study. The verified cash-price application executes that signal code
without adding the separate study stop. Original futures contracts,
rolls, dollar stops, financing, borrow, slippage and intraday stop paths
remain unresolved and held. No USD number is reinterpreted as INR. No
original book statistics are used as computed returns.

do not inherit admission. They are variants of SineTrend, not additional

## Independent source review on the prepared base

The independent reviewer inspected the original Rocket Science spreads at
PDF pages 62–64 (printed 119–123), the original S&C article at PDF pages 3–4,
the companion MAMA article, the Linear Prediction, Time Warp and Market Mode
articles, and all five supplied software manuals. Rocket Science p.119 supports
