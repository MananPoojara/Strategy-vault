# Radge: plain session open-to-close index research

Only `radge_oc_long` is reviewed here. Nick Radge's *Successful Stock Trading* (2012), PDF page 17 / printed page 16, gives an unconditional entry at each session open and an exit at that session close. It has no indicator, stop, target, overnight holding or selection rule. Source PDF SHA256: `6f4a3940ec761bcb1de15c5bc2e04482e71d171aa688a8769203e5404ef0f96d`.

This is a declared price-return research application to actual supplied NIFTY 50 and policy-qualified SPX index observations. It does not assert executable cash-index fills, infer futures contracts or quantities, or replace stocks, ETFs or derivatives with an index. The original unnamed index, undated 5.5-year study and source performance are not reproduced. Its printed gross gain less gross loss is 16.39 while its printed net is 16.56; the implementation is not tuned toward either value.

For each eligible actual session, gross return is `(close-open)/open`. The platform deducts user-entered fees and slippage once per filled side as normalized return charges. There is no inferred statutory tax schedule or futures rollover. An observed open equal to close remains a closed flat-gross trade; it still incurs any modeled charges. Equality alone neither proves nor disproves opening-price authenticity. The Nifty 2000 minimum and SPX 2014 opening-data cutoff remain binding.

An independent source-based calculation checked all 21,990 configuration records across six 0/5-bps cases: complete Nifty 2000–2026, Nifty 2010–2020 and qualified SPX 2014–2020. Comparing those records to both existing targets produced 43,980 row comparisons with no mismatch. Those overlapping cost/window cases are one method, with 8,341 unique instrument/date observations. The source CSVs, executable receipts and target ledger hashes were checked. Vendor authenticity and complete official-session calendar coverage were not certified.

Net Profit is the sum of actual closed-trade returns and equals the unrounded average times the closed count. Sequential compounded return is a separately labelled product of chronological return factors. The negative complete Nifty result is retained; no tuning, fabricated price or removal of losing sessions is used to match a favorable source illustration.

The two fixed-point stop methods, `radge_oc_long_stop` and `radge_oc_short_stop`, remain held. Their unnamed source point units and intraday stop execution do not inherit this approval. The separate course/futures method `radge_ma220` also receives no admission. Normal data, calculation, symbol and instrument guards continue to apply.
