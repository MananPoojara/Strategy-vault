## What is established

| Component | Pages | Source information | Review boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chart framework | 10–11 | Stock charts at 5-, 15- and 60-minute intervals; simple moving averages of 20 and 200 observations; volume | These are bar periods, rather than an instruction to use 20 or 200 calendar days for every chart. |
| Candle direction | 12–13 | Candle colour represents the relationship between open and close; six candle concepts describe changing momentum | Momentum interpretation alone supplies no complete entry/exit system. |
| Pristine Buy/Sell Setup | 14–17 | Consecutive directional candles with new opens near prior closes, followed by a reversal; overlapping congestion is excluded | The illustrations do not supply a numeric proximity/overlap rule or a universal order and exit contract. |
| Multi-timeframe bias | 18–20 | A 60-minute price relative to its SMA20 guides direction; 5-/15-minute charts locate entries. Countertrend examples combine an extended higher-timeframe move with a lower-timeframe SMA20 transition | The qualitative extension condition, exact evaluation time and synchronization/order decisions require further source work. |
| Changing of the Guard | 23, dependency only | At least three consecutive bearish candles followed by a bullish candle; reverse the colours for its bearish counterpart | This is an objectively recognizable pattern component. It must not be silently equated with every condition of the Pristine Buy/Sell Setup. |
| Risk and target examples | 36 and 59, dependencies only | An illustrated risk boundary below a COG; potential resistance/MA200 targets and a five-minute example with a bearish 60-minute context | These slides do not, by themselves, settle a universal entry, initial/dynamic target, stop execution or same-bar ordering policy. |

Page 14 allows the setup pattern on different bar intervals. That statement
does not establish that the full 60-minute/5-minute trading framework can be
reproduced by substituting daily bars. A daily COG study would be a separate
component study, without an invented trade exit or performance claim.

contains useful objective components; it has not been dismissed as psychology.
