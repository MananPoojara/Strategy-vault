These are useful source rules, but **this bounded review has not established
a complete mechanical protocol**. A stop based on the eventual low or high
of an entry bar can introduce lookahead if that extreme was unknown when
the order was placed. The first-30-minute alternatives also need session
prices; daily bars cannot establish their execution path. Same-bar order,
stop and target interactions require a declared execution convention after
the core rules have been fixed.

A small offset within the printed range, an indicator seed, or an optional
filter choice can be stated as an implementation convention. Selecting an
unstated mandatory smoothness threshold, swing detector and exit branch
would instead create a mechanical adaptation requiring its own explicit
contract. No such adaptation or performance claim is added here.

The source's dollar offsets and dollar partial-profit amounts must not be
silently applied to rupee prices. A future implementation requires actual
stocks, the appropriate timeframe and a reviewed quote/adjustment basis;
an index cannot stand in for stock orders. This source review makes no
claim that the necessary data are missing or already qualified.
