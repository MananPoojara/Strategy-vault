## What the complete read establishes

The manual explicitly identifies its moving averages as simple averages on printed page 141. It permits 40/50-period interchangeability. Its change-of-guard example on printed page 101 uses three to five opposite-colour candles followed by a reversal candle. Those details must remain specific to this manual when interpreting a setup.

The daily climactic-buy examples on printed pages 173–174 give an extension threshold of at least 8% from the 20-period moving average. The intraday examples use a different threshold. The section provides entry and stop alternatives, a moving-average target and management after two completed bars. A daily research contract needs to state the selected alternatives, the observation used to measure extension, and causal order handling. Required trailing management must be preserved.

The bullish gap-surprise section on printed pages 267–268 provides numeric range, candle-location and gap conditions, an opening-entry alternative, a prior-day stop and profit/time-exit alternatives. Its omitted volume-comparison period can be a clearly declared research default. The absolute dollar thresholds also require the appropriate quotation and adjustment basis in the stock data.

These two setups are candidates for explicit daily stock research contracts. Each contract still needs independent source review, data qualification, implementation and trade-level checks before results become available.
