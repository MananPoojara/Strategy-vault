## Corrections to an overly restrictive interpretation

A numeric interval is a usable constraint. The cup's 20–30% depth is not unusable just because the text does not select one endpoint. The material gaps concern how the pattern is identified causally and how the resulting position is managed.

A missing indicator seed, averaging convention, monetary capital model, fee schedule or printed trade list is not automatically a reason to reject a normalized research application. Minor computational choices may be declared and tested. This source's hold rests on missing decisions that would change which stocks or trades are selected, their intraday ordering and their mandatory exits. Supplying an invented exit would create a new strategy.

ADX 30 in the general prose and 25 in a particular example are a source discrepancy. The example may reflect a different setup; neither value is certified as a universal rule by this review. Two explicitly labelled source variants could be investigated after the remaining required conditions and data are established. Selecting the more profitable threshold would not resolve the source question.

Three five-minute bars and three ten-minute bars imply different waiting periods. A daily bar cannot show which events happened after either wait, or whether the cited gap and tick sequence occurred. The upper-quarter closing-range condition is only part of an optional holding decision; it does not supply the omitted market-climate/trend rules or establish a compulsory fixed exit after three or five days.
