# Kaufman TSM4: bounded source and trade-logic review

Seventeen distinct price-based methods from **Perry J. Kaufman's New Trading Systems and Methods, Fourth Edition (2005)** and its supplied companion CD have been reviewed against local sources and independently checked on actual supplied OHLC data. This review supports the stated rule interpretations and their computed trade ledgers. It does not reproduce or certify the original historical studies, contract-dollar results, vendor price adjustments, or portfolio results.

The source is the supplied 942-page Books24x7 PDF, `Books/Perry Kaufman/New Trading Systems and Methods 4th Edition Incl CD (Perry Kaufman).pdf`, and relevant text files under `Books/Perry Kaufman/TSM4 CD`. Page references below are **PDF pages**. The private evidence report records source and market-file SHA-256 digests. Neither source PDFs/text extracts nor raw market files are committed to the public repository.

## Corrections and declared assumptions

The two Hochheimer moving-average implementations previously entered whenever prices/averages were above or below their comparison line. The source requires crossing events and prohibits execution on a high=low day. This difference can create an entry on the following day even though no new crossing occurred. Both runners now use actual crossings, skip the ineligible day, and wait for another eligible crossing. Focused regression tests cover that case. The modified-three-average CD strategy keeps its state comparisons because that is what its supplied code says.

Parameter validation now rejects unknown keys, nonfinite values, out-of-range values and fractional bar counts before converting or allocating windows. The faster Hochheimer MA must remain shorter than the slower one. This bounds direct calls as well as documented search settings.

The exponential CD system supplies period0, which is not executable as a smoothing period. Its adjustable20-bar default is a **research assumption**, borrowed from the companion comparisons. The CD calls the platform `SmoothedAverage` function without providing initialization history or MaxBarsBack settings. The engine's full-window mean seed and alpha1/period are a declared convention, not certified original-platform parity. Neither default is a recovered missing author optimum or evidence of original-study reproduction.

Every test starts flat at its selected start. Prior actual bars warm indicators. One full position per symbol is tracked; no overlapping positions or portfolio sizing are inferred. Existing positions at the last bar remain **open**, so their marks are excluded from closed-trade counts and the sum/mean of closed-trade returns. Source systems that reverse remain continuously invested after their first eligible entry, subject to that explicit start convention.

Close-based fills follow the supplied CD code where specified. The Hochheimer prose and seasonal descriptions omit the time of day; close fills are an explicitly declared adaptation. The Bollinger system retains its specified next-open fills. No future price is used to generate an entry.

The seasonal sections refer to broad stock-market/index studies, explicitly including the S&P index; the Hirsch discussion also mentions ETFs. They support **index/actual-ETF research application**. Private constituent-stock cases below test arithmetic and state transitions only; those cases do not establish that the source supplied a stock-selection method. They do not authorize publishing a basket portfolio or claiming stock-universe study reproduction.

Calendar rules use the supplied observed trading-date sequence to locate month boundaries. This assumes those dates represent the exchange calendar; missing vendor sessions are not invented. The Hirsch calculation excludes dividend reinvestment, interest on uninvested cash, taxes and leverage. Cash-index short trades are hypothetical price exposure without a futures basis, contract roll, funding or borrow model. Actual ETF and constituent-stock prices remain their own instruments.

## Independent verification

The independent audit executable is [verify_source_batch.py](scripts/verify_source_batch.py). It compiles the **actual** `kftsm.rs` runner and `connors.rs` context/indicator implementation into a private command-line harness. A separate Python oracle expresses the reviewed source predicates with40-digit Decimal arithmetic and its own position ledger.

The bounded audit covered **68 cases:17 methods ×4 real supplied series**: Nifty50 cash index (India), SPY ETF (US), RELIANCE stock (India), and AAPL stock (US). The selected period was January2010 through September11,2020, with each series retaining its earlier actual warm-up bars; Nifty data before2000 was excluded. The actual first/last trading dates and file digests are recorded per case. These cases validate instrument-specific trade logic, not portfolio performance or the original source populations.

**All7,472 ledger records matched**, with zero differences in row counts, directions, entry/exit bar indices, entry/exit prices within the declared floating-point tolerance, fill kinds, or terminal-open status. Open records are included in that verification count and excluded from closed-trade statistics. Six focused Rust tests passed. There are no fabricated price fixtures in the real-data ledger audit; small synthetic candles are used only in focused boundary regression tests.

Original market inputs were read only and their file size/modification timestamps were checked after the audit. Private generated artifacts are under `/home/server/.cache/strategy-vault-webapp/overnight-2026-10-13/kaufman/`. API admission and deployment remain separate reviewed steps; a successful private harness run does not silently enable a method in the public UI.

## Remaining limits and next review

The book's original contract-dollar results, commissions, position sizes, study date ranges and membership are not reproduced by these new-market tests. Vendor corporate-action adjustments and point-in-time membership also remain uncertified. Historical fitted parameters can be explored but are not promises of future performance. No source-book numbers are pasted into computed charts or statistics.

Other Kaufman runners remain pending independent source review. The next useful bounded batches are: the TSM4 adaptive-driver systems with their function files; outside-day stop/time ordering; and *Smarter Trading* Chapters5,6,8. Intraday, delivery-month, event-series and incomplete discretionary methods retain their own data/rule requirements. No blanket exemption is created for futures, options, stock-selection universes or portfolio sizing.
