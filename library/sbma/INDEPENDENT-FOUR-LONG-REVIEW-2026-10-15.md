# Moving Averages 101: four selected-stock long/cash versions

This bounded review covers `sbma_x_15e30e`, `sbma_x_15e150e`, `sbma_x_10s200s` and `sbma_x_50s200s`: four additional period/type versions of the existing crossover method, with zero new algorithms. The five previously reviewed Burns stock versions retain their existing behavior. The 200-SMA short, 10/30 EMA short-reversal rule and RSI-dependent or discretionary rules remain held. Code/catalogue registration is separate from the independent host review and release decision.

Steve and Holly Burns, *Moving Averages 101* (2015), physical PDF58–60 and67 support an upward shorter/longer average cross for entry and the opposite cross for exit. PDF60 lists 15/30 EMA, 15/150 EMA, 10/200 SMA and 50/200 SMA; PDF64 identifies the Golden Cross. The reviewed source scope is physical PDF9–18 and54–67, not the whole book. No method-specific printed trade/statistics table was identified in this scope; the QQQ/SPY illustrations and an original historical stock universe or portfolio are not reproduced. The source PDF's SHA256 is `5d8fc8a50825df615f3285131ecb30f644609d771fbf90252caa7338a1388d40`.

These are declared individual-stock research applications on actual prices. Completed daily closes determine the cross. Enter long when fast is strictly above slow after previously at or below; exit to cash when fast is strictly below slow after previously at or above. Equality holds, and the test begins flat and waits for a fresh cross. Always-in short reversal is excluded. No RSI, MACD, earnings, stock-selection, volatility filter or capital sizing rule is inferred.

SMA is the mean of the last n supplied closes. EMA alpha2/(n+1), the first-n SMA seed, strict ties and flat test boundaries are implementation conventions; the book does not specify EMA initialization. All actual supplied earlier history remains loaded. An independent first-price seed sensitivity did not change the selected-window signals, which is not universal seed equivalence. Shortening the warmup to200 earlier observations changed six RELIANCE EMA150 signal dates in2010 (February10/19, March16/17 and June16/17), so a tail-truncated or reset/reseeded run cannot inherit this proof. Both current and prior averages require real earlier bars; invalid earlier OHLC is held rather than discarded.

PDF16 and63 provide completed-close and next-day execution context. Fill0 is idealized signal-close research, not a mandatory book default or evidence of an attainable closing fill. Fill1 uses the next actual observed opening price. Terminal next-open orders stay unfilled; an already entered position remains open. There are no invented candles or forced exit orders. Only exact period/type defaults, long/cash and these two fills are in scope. HTTP parsing and optimization reject malformed, unknown, nonfinite or altered parameters only for these four IDs; legacy method parsing remains unchanged. Both reviewed fill choices may be compared, while unreviewed periods and directions cannot be optimized through this admission.

The independent reference covers AAPL2010-01-04–2020-09-11 (2,692 selected sessions;7,331 earlier rows) and RELIANCE2010-01-04–2020-12-31 (2,730 selected sessions;2,496 earlier rows). AAPL's source stops in September; the native route must reject an end beyond its actual coverage rather than clip a December request silently. The repaired Indian v2 source is explicitly bound to this proof. MSFT and HDFCBANK were input diagnostics only. No cash index, ETF, future or option substitutes for the two selected stocks. Price adjustments, dividends, historical membership, calendar/suspension coverage and executable opening prices remain qualified; selected-stock correctness does not certify a historical basket.

Costs are deductions per filled side from normalized allocation, not statutory taxes, monetary sizing or fees on changing exit notional. A gross-unit return model may leave residual cash equal to the entry fee; it is not a funded capital account. That terminal estimate is separately reconciled and does not create a filled exit. Closed Net Profit excludes open marks; separately labelled compounded equity includes them. Gross returns, fees/slippage and net allocation must reconcile under the unchanged platform accounting.
