# Moving Averages 101: three long/cash admissions and a held short reference

Steve and Holly Burns, *Moving Averages 101* (2015). This source/ledger review covers exactly `sbma_sma200_short`, `sbma_x_5e50s`, `sbma_x_10e20e` and `sbma_x_10e50e` at their source-listed default periods and directions. These are four configurations across two rule families, not four new independent algorithms. **This admission candidate enables only the three long/cash configurations. `sbma_sma200_short` remains held by instrument policy pending its diagnostic-display correction; its arithmetic evidence is preserved as a private reference.** Other Burns methods and nondefault parameter changes do not inherit this review.

## Source rules and explicit conventions

Physical PDF54–57, especially56, describes shorting the first loss of200SMA with a close back above that average as the cover. The reviewed rule requires a completed close below200SMA after the prior close was at/above its prior average. Equality holds. The optional volatility-dependent5/10EMA trailing stop remains excluded rather than silently selecting one. This is a hypothetical stock-price-return application: actual borrowing, margin, overnight short permission, financing and loan costs are not established by historical OHLC.

Physical PDF58–60 and67 define shorter/longer-average crossover entry and exit and explicitly list5EMA/50SMA,10EMA/20EMA and10EMA/50EMA. These reviewed defaults are long/cash: current fast>slow after prior fast≤slow enters, current fast<slow after prior fast≥slow exits. Equality alone holds. No optional RSI, MACD, volatility, earnings or stock-selection filter is invented. The PCLN discretionary RSI example does not replace these generic crossover exits.

The book does not state an EMA initialization formula. Alpha2/(n+1), seeded with the mean of the firstn completed closes, is a declared implementation convention. Full supplied earlier history initializes indicators; the selected window begins flat. A fresh independent reference also computed a first-observed-price seed sensitivity on the actual histories. All selected-window order lists were unchanged in these samples; this does not establish seed independence for arbitrary truncated histories or other data.

PDF16 supports completed-close decisions and next-day use/next-open exits. Fill0 is an idealized same-close research assumption, not a guaranteed executable closing auction or a mandatory printed default. Fill1 uses the next actual observed open. A terminal next-open order stays unfilled; an open position is not given an invented exit.

Use actual individual-stock prices. AAPL and RELIANCE are explicit selected-stock research applications, not reproductions of SPY/QQQ/PCLN illustrations or an original stock population. Indian stocks are a declared market application. Relative-price indicators here have no nominalUSD price threshold. Current snapshots, vendor prices and corporate-action histories retain their existing provenance limitations.
