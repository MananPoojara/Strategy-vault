## Declared, fixed research conventions

1. Use one actual admitted stock account at a time (AAPL, RELIANCE); no index or ETF substitutes. Inputs are actual supplied daily OHLC; stock volume is present throughout the selected2010–2020 windows, although Impulse/SafeZone do not consume it. These two selections do not reproduce discretionary active-stock/channel selection or a historical basket.
2. EMA has alpha2/(N+1), first N actual values averaged as its seed, then recursive updates. No invented prehistory. At least two finite completed weekly EMA26 readings and the daily EMA13/MACD12-26-9 states precede entry. The account starts flat at the chosen test start, without an inherited pending order; the first selected close can signal the following actual open. Indicators retain actual earlier observations. Missing required history is a hold, not a filled price.
3. Calendar weeks run Monday–Sunday. At an actual next-open event, commit only prior calendar weeks before reading that bar's OHLC. Include all actually observed prior-week sessions, including NSE weekend sessions; the final available close of each such week is the weekly close. Do not infer completion from a future row or declare a terminal midweek bar complete. The first observed weekly bar uses only actual observations; no earlier price is filled. This execution-clock completed-week convention is explicit research metadata, not a printed original setting.
4. A daily Impulse signal uses the preceding observed close. Entry at the next observed open requires current completed-week EMA26 direction to agree. Exit when the prior daily Impulse signal disappears; the weekly indicator is the directional entry filter. The primary Study Guide describes buy/sell clusters from the TWO daily indicators while the weekly trend adds weight. No new divergence, channel, discretionary stock-selection or opposite-signal rule is invented. A weekly change alone does not become a fabricated daily Impulse cross; any alternative exit interpretation stays unadmitted.
5. No pyramiding; at most one position. Entries are considered only from a flat prior close. Thus an open-position exit does not produce an automatic same-open reversal; a later flat-close signal can re-enter. This sequencing convention is declared, not recovered original account behavior.
6. The initial SafeZone protection is computed from data through the prior close and is active immediately after a market-at-open entry. Intraday stop-entry + stop ambiguity is avoided because source-offered MARKET entry is used for Impulse; the Triple Screen stop entry is never replaced with a market order. If current open is already on the wrong side of initial protection or no valid noise average exists, do not enter an unprotected trade. While held, retain the existing protection when a new noise average is undefined; never loosen a stop. Gap through an existing stop fills at actual open; otherwise a touched known stop fills at that level. Continuous research stop levels and supplied adjustment basis are declared; broker tick/lot/borrow availability is not certified.
7. Account equity begins at normalized1, with cash, a reserved principal for shorts, and actual held-position price marks. Fractional research units and cash-only exposure capped at1 account unit are declared; no monetary capital, INR/USD profit, real share-lot or borrowed-stock availability is claimed. Shorts are hypothetical price exposure. Actual money/quantity fields remain absent/null.
8. Per-side fee/slippage bps follow the platform's explicitly labelled normalized entry-notional deduction. Both expected entry and exit costs must fit inside planned risk. For a new trade with equity E, monthly reference B and protective distance d=abs(open-stop)/open, deployment fraction w is capped at1/(1+side_rate), so entry notional plus entry costs fit available cash, and by R/(E*(d+2*side_rate)), where R=min(.02*B, remaining .06*B monthly risk). This is a maximum planned risk ceiling; gaps can lose more, as source p171 expressly explains. No cost parameter is silently ignored in source sizing.
9. At a new month before the first open, reference B becomes the preceding observed closing account equity (including actual open marks and costs already paid). The monthly realized-loss allowance resets; an existing holding's monthly basis becomes that prior mark. Recalibrate its protection, never loosening it, so prospective loss from the new monthly basis plus estimated exit cost fits .02*B. Old entry fees are already included in B; do not charge them again as new-month risk.
10. Track current-month realized losses without replenishment by winning trades. For an old holding carried over month-end, loss for the new month is measured from the previous month-end mark plus costs paid in the new month. New entries must keep realized losses plus outstanding planned risk within .06*B. Also inspect actual closing marked equity: below .94*B queues liquidation at the next actual open and prohibits new trades for the rest of that month. Daily-close inspection and next-open liquidation are explicit causal execution conventions; no intraday account threshold order or same-close hindsight fill is assumed. A new month resets entry eligibility after any already-required liquidation; no immediate reversal is invented.
11. Open marks deduct actual entry fees only; they never charge a closing fee for a transaction that has not occurred. An unfilled terminal liquidation/order remains pending, and a terminal holding stays open. Gross comparison must retain the SAME accepted quantities/fills and remove actual costs, with no independent gross re-selection. Full equity, cash, held quantities, realized-loss and risk-budget conservation must reconcile; a signal-only percentage product is insufficient.
