SOURCE COVERAGE CONTRACT v1 (job context v2; this source task only)

The source_cursor context names your exact private source-coverage.json path and
actor job ID. Write this sidecar even when every selected method remains held.
Task completion is not whole-source completion. Read actual source identity and
contents, enumerate all known chapter/section boundaries and preserve every
unread section and every additional method beyond the three-method batch cap.
Do not infer objective rules from headings. A document may be an article, manual,
partial scan or duplicate edition. Use the source's actual instrument/frequency.

Sidecar JSON shape (all examples below describe generated schema, not book data):

Use actual physical PDF pages starting at1. Two-page spreads can use disjoint
left/right regions; whole overlaps either half. Source section page ownership
must not overlap. Shared context can be cited in evidence without claiming a
second source section owns it. Missing source sections have no invented physical
pages: pages:[] plus explicit missing_printed_pages and partial/unverified work.
An inventory marked complete must cover every actual physical page, including
front matter/end matter. This still does not certify a complete authored book.

Read every page of a bounded section before marking it read. Bind locally existing
source-page extraction/render/checklist evidence to its actual hash and page cells.
Evidence paths are relative to this phase's own artifact directory, never parent
traversal, absolute paths or symlinks. All read section cells need evidence.
If prior accepted coverage exists, preserve its stable section/method identities,
page cells, reviewed states and genuine holds; use its accepted evidence binding
when retaining cumulative reading. Do not erase earlier chapters or reopen held
methods without a separately reviewed changed-input/repair contract. Unread and
unreadable areas remain explicit. Inventory all discovered methods/components;
at most three batch_method_ids may be investigated/implemented in this batch.
Extra methods remain pending; held methods keep precise reasons. Parameter
variants and algorithm families stay distinct. source_checked means source-only,
not numerical correctness, integrated readiness, original study reproduction or
public admission. Reading-only material cannot become an invented strategy.

For REVIEW: independently read the actual source before candidate calculations,
validate the converter's frozen sidecar and your own source coverage, and write
your own sidecar/evidence under the review job. Add top-level accepted:true only
if you accept the same logical source/section/method coverage, plus
converter_sidecar_sha256 equal to the exact original converter sidecar SHA in the
frozen binding file. Your job_id must be the distinct review ID. On disagreement
write accepted:false with evidence; never copy an unsupported pass. Converter
coverage must remain unchanged during review. Each phase retains separate paths.

The coordinator validates actual PDF SHA/page count, page bounds, evidence paths
and hashes, independent actor/binding agreement and monotonic accepted coverage.
Only an accepted pair can update the private source cursor and produce bounded
residual child PROPOSALS. No child is automatically appended or publicly admitted.
Preserve source/rule/data holds and all losing results. A completed three-method
job must leave all other chapters/methods visible in the cursor.
