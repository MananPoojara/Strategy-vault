# MACS actual-stock price research

Kestner's Quantitative Trading Strategies (2003), printed p. 207, describes twenty moving-average relationships. The detailed prose admits long scores at or above 60 and short scores at or below 40; the stricter summary remains a disclosed interpretation conflict. The named profile uses close-based simple averages with lengths n and 4n for n = 1..20. Its score is five times the number of relationships with the shorter average strictly above the longer average. Pair equality contributes zero long votes under a declared daily-relationship convention. The book's description of systems on long trades does not uniquely resolve stateful ties, averaging initialization or every execution detail. The selected interpretation is not presented as a performance-selected resolution.

Votes compare four times the exact last-n RAW native-decimal close sum with the exact last-4n sum. The typed loader binds these operands to the same file bytes, hash, observation ordinal, date and ingested OHLC used for the selected stock. A rounded Rust floating-point display is not a substitute for those raw decimal votes. Supplied centered binary64 means are approximate diagnostics and cannot determine pair flags. Execution prices and ordinary account values retain the existing binary64 ingestion/accounting model; exact-decimal vote arithmetic does not imply exact-decimal financial accounting.

A complete state requires 80 genuine closes: first state at source ordinal 79 and earliest next-open fill 80. The full genuine earlier history supplies warmup while the selected account starts flat. Recurring eligible levels, completed-close decisions, next actual stock open fills, opposite-entry exit/reversal, no pyramiding and terminal open close marks are declared research conventions. A final signal cannot fill outside the selected dates. A terminal mark is not a filled exit; it stays outside closed-trade statistics and retains a separate estimated liquidation-cost label.

The fixed sample consists of AAPL from 1990-01-01 through 2001-12-31 and RELIANCE from 2001-01-01 through 2010-12-31, each at fees/slippage 0/0 and 3/2 bps per side, rollover 0. For each of MACS and TIAR, four ordinary normalized-account cases and 16 direct production-function responses matched unchanged source/account references with zero mismatches: 32 responses and eight accounts combined. Compared responses were detail, data readiness, selected summary and a singleton optimizer. Selected daily states, exact MACS pair decisions, source-event/fill/terminal clocks, closed/open ledgers, daily account returns/equity and statistics were within that sample's comparison scope. Earlier complete-prefix source evidence has separate scope from selected API states. Source/reference/account-adapter authors share implementation context, so these are mechanical corroboration, not blind independent replication. Earlier failed checks retain their original scope; they are not relabelled by a later passing artifact.

Only server-qualified actual individual stocks are admitted to this research path. Index, ETF, derivative, basket and client-spoofed native identities are not substitutes. Long 60 / short 40 are exact fixed defaults; raw request parsing and singleton grids reject alternative settings before floating-point rounding or underflow. Profile and numeric/source conventions are server-owned. Generic canonical MACS calls cannot fall back to rounded-price votes. Global summary and optimizer segments use their own actual selected endpoint and verified raw prefix. Stock baskets and simultaneous portfolio capacity are unsupported.

Readiness concerns source and data; it does not certify financial support. Every other requested window still needs its own source/data/account guards. Nonpositive or nonfinite normalized accounts remain unavailable without resetting equity, clipping observations, changing dates or sizing, or inventing funding. HTTP transport/request-matrix, authenticated browser, export, deployment and measured library availability are separate acceptance gates; direct function comparison does not prove those gates.
