# Three in a row (TIAR) actual-stock price research

Kestner's Quantitative Trading Strategies (2003), printed pp. 222 and 227, supplies the fixed strict close-level rule. Long requires close(t)>close(t−5)>close(t−10)>close(t−15); short requires the reverse strict chain. These are recurring levels, not observed crossings or three consecutive daily returns. Equality provides no new direction. Spacing counts genuine source observations, not calendar days. The first complete state is source ordinal 15 and the earliest next actual open fill is 16: sixteen genuine closes are required.

Full genuine earlier history supplies warmup, while the selected account starts flat. Completed-close decisions, next actual stock open fills and opposite-entry exit/reversal are declared execution conventions. Repeated same-side levels do not pyramid. A terminal signal does not fill outside the selected window. An existing position is marked at the final selected close and remains outside closed-trade statistics. Ordinary accounting separately labels an estimated exit-cost side on an open mark; it is not an actual filled exit or monetary sizing claim.

The fixed sample consists of AAPL from 1990-01-01 through 2001-12-31 and RELIANCE from 2001-01-01 through 2010-12-31, each at fees/slippage 0/0 and 3/2 bps per side, rollover 0. For each of TIAR and MACS, four ordinary normalized accounts and 16 direct production-function responses matched unchanged source/account references with zero mismatches: 32 responses and eight accounts combined. Compared responses were detail, data readiness, selected summary and singleton optimizer. The sample covered selected daily causal states, source-event clocks, closed/open trade ledgers, daily equity/returns and compared statistics. Earlier preselection complete-prefix source evidence is separately qualified; a selected report is not a fresh capture of every earlier API state. Shared reference/source/account-adapter authorship and implementation exposure mean mechanical corroboration, not blind independent replication. Earlier source/binary/comparison proofs and failed artifacts retain their original scope.

The exact default is step = 5. Raw settings and singleton optimizer grids reject alternatives before floating-point rounding can collapse a different decimal to that default. Unknown or duplicate/malformed protected fields and client source-profile overrides are rejected. Actual-stock identity comes from the server whitelist/native evidence, not a request flag. Index, ETF, derivative and basket substitutions are excluded. Whole-file native loader integrity and selected-prefix OHLC/ordinal/date checks remain separate; a valid prefix does not waive whole-file rules. Global summary and optimizer subwindows use the same stock rules with their own selected start/end and ordinary account guards.

Supplied causal states expose full-precision close and lag 5/10/15 anchors with the exact decision booleans. Entry and exit signal clocks identify the completed close preceding an actual open fill. The legacy trade signal remains an exit/terminal observation field and is not relabelled as entry signal. Missing states or inconsistent trade clocks suppress a report. No warmup, missing quote, open fill, terminal exit or borrow availability is invented.

Data readiness does not prove a positive supported account. Other requested runs need their own source/data/account checks. Unsupported nonpositive or nonfinite equity remains unavailable without resets, clipping, date changes, sizing changes or invented funding. HTTP transport/request-matrix, authenticated browser, export, deployment and measured library availability are separate acceptance gates; direct production-function checks do not establish those gates.
