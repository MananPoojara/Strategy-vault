# Kestner: Second Order Breakout stock research

Source: Lars Kestner, *Quantitative Trading Strategies* (2003), printed pages185 and190–191. Method ID `lkqts_sob` belongs to book `lkqts`. This implementation is a declared individual-stock percentage-return application. Original published performance and volatility-based dollar quantities are not reproduced.

The fixed profile is `sob_close_max80_current_inclusive_channel40_tie_inclusive_strict20`, with search80, channel40 and minimum20. For each completed daily close, find the longest current-inclusive high and low horizons within80 closes. Price ties count toward a horizon. Use the high horizon positively when it is longer, the low horizon negatively when it is longer, and zero when they are equal. Over40 complete statistic observations, go long when the statistic is strictly above20 and equals its channel high; go short when strictly below−20 and equal to its channel low. Ties at the channel extreme qualify. Exactly±20 does not qualify.

The formal entry paragraph on page190 uses strict thresholds; the Intel example on page191 uses exactly±20. This named interpretation follows the formal paragraph and retains that conflict. Current-inclusive windows, price/channel tie handling, equal-horizon neutrality and complete initialization are declared conventions. The first statistic is observation79, the first complete channel predicate118 and the earliest possible next-open entry119, using zero-based source indices.

Signals use the completed close and fill at the next actual observation's open. Chapter7 page139 supplies the earlier study's next-open context; applying it to this Chapter8 method is a declared adaptation. Selected exposure starts flat while genuine earlier history supplies indicators. Repeated same-direction entries do not add exposure. An opposite signal exits and reverses at the same next open; there is no independent stop. A terminal holding has a close mark and remains open. No order is filled after the selected end.

Only server-qualified actual individual stocks are admitted. A stock constituent label must not substitute an index, ETF, derivative or basket portfolio. Quote adjustments, complete exchange calendars, historical membership, dividends, borrow, margin and financing remain unverified. Required source/history and accounting checks apply separately to every request. Data readiness alone does not prove an economically valid account; a nonpositive normalized account remains unavailable rather than being clipped or reset.

Fees and slippage are separate user inputs in basis points per side of deployed normalized allocation. Slippage is a return deduction and does not rewrite native fill prices. Rollover requires verified contract-event mapping; this stock profile does not invent rolls. Open-position equity retains the ordinary report's disclosed estimated liquidation charges. Closed-trade Net Profit excludes open marks; compounded equity and gross/net ledgers retain their separately described accounting basis. No monetary P&L, statutory tax schedule or funded portfolio is implied.

Backend diagnostics expose actual source-indexed signed horizons, channel bounds, strict decision flags and separate completed-close, next-open fill and terminal-mark clocks. Values before complete initialization are unavailable. The frontend must display supplied states rather than recomputing source signals. Synthetic source and integration tests are data-independent; they neither recover an original published trade ledger nor certify market performance.
