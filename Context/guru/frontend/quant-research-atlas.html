Quant Research Atlas

Research and books only, in the spirit of Quantpedia: strategy research databases, books and publishers, academic papers and journals, and research blogs, from the US and Europe to India, Japan and Brazil. Every link was checked live on 2 October 2026.

How this list was made. Researched with Exa web search and GitHub, then expanded from curated lists (Quantocracy's bloggers, QuantInsti's guide to quant websites, Feedspot, Alpha Architect, Setup4Alpha). Only websites are listed: GitHub repositories are excluded. Every URL was then requested on 2 October 2026: live, live but blocks automated checks (403/429), could not be confirmed at the time of checking. Dead links were removed. Only research and book websites are listed: trading platforms, forums, courses, data vendors and podcasts are excluded. "Freemium" means a useful free tier plus paid features.
